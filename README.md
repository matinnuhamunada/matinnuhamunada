## Hi, I'm Matin 👋

**CTO at [Integrated Genome Factory](https://igf-sequencing.com)** | **Assistant Professor at [Universitas Gadjah Mada](https://ugm.ac.id)**

I work where genomics meets software engineering: building sequencing and bioinformatics infrastructure in industry, and teaching bioinformatics, biotechnology, and computational biology at the Faculty of Biology, UGM.

### 🧬 What I do
- 🏭 **At IGF**: I lead the technology behind our genome sequencing services, including data pipelines, compute infrastructure, and turning raw reads into results clients can use.
- 🎓 **At UGM**: I teach bioinformatics, biotechnology, and systems biology, and I supervise students working on genomics and bioinformatics.
- 🔬 **Research interests**: microbial genomics, biosynthetic gene clusters, pangenomics, and reproducible workflows.

### 🛠️ Projects
- 💬 Ask me about [**BGCFlow**](https://github.com/NBChub/bgcflow), a systematic workflow for analysing biosynthetic gene clusters across large genome collections (pangenomes).
- 🌱 Currently exploring **agentic retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)** and **knowledge graphs** to aid genome mining.
